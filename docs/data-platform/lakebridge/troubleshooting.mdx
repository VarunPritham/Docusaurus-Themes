## Transpile fails on QUALIFY clauses

<Panel type="error" title="Symptom">
  `TranspileError: unsupported construct QUALIFY at line 14`
</Panel>

You are on the generic profile. Re-run:

```bash
lakebridge configure --transpiler-profile td-firm-v3
```

<Expand title="Other known transpile gaps">
  BTEQ control logic and journal tables are flagged as *manual rewrite* in the
  assessment report — they are not transpiled automatically.
</Expand>
